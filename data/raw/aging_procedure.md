# Aging Procedure for Formed Diaphragms

This procedure applies to diaphragms formed from cold-rolled cobalt alloy strip.

## Preparation

Clean parts in an alkaline wash and rinse them before loading, so that no forming lubricant remains on the surface. Load parts on clean fixtures that do not touch the active diaphragm area.

## Furnace Cycle

Age the parts in a vacuum furnace or under a protective atmosphere. Record the furnace run number on the lot traveller so the heat treatment can be traced later.

## Cooling and Release

Cool the parts in the furnace atmosphere before exposing them to air. Measure the hardness of sample parts from each furnace run before the lot is released.
