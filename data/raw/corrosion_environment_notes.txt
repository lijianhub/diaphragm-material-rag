Co-Cr-Ni-Mo alloys form a thin chromium-rich oxide film that protects them in seawater, body fluids and many industrial chemicals.

Chloride environments can cause pitting in weaker alloys. The molybdenum content of ELGILOY improves resistance to pitting and crevice corrosion.

Hydrogen embrittlement can occur when high-strength metal parts absorb hydrogen during plating or cathodic protection. Parts are baked after plating to drive out absorbed hydrogen.

Diaphragms used in sensors for corrosive media are often supplied passivated, a treatment that cleans the surface and restores the protective oxide film after machining.
