Every production lot is assigned a lot number that links the finished diaphragms to the strip coil, the forming press and the heat treatment run.

Inspection records list the measured profile height, flatness and thickness for a sample of parts from each lot. Records are kept for the lifetime of the product so that failures can be traced.

When a part fails in service, a failure analysis examines the fracture surface. Beach marks on the fracture surface indicate fatigue, while a rough, fibrous surface suggests overload.

A nonconformance report is raised when a lot fails inspection. The report records the defect, the root cause and the corrective action before the lot can be released or scrapped.
